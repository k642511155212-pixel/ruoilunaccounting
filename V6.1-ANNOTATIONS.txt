Accounting Mastery v0.6.1 — Annotation Mode

New personal study layer:
- Select text in any Learn lesson to open an annotation toolbar.
- Four highlight types: Important, Exam Trap, Understood/Example, Definition.
- Add/edit/delete inline comments attached to highlighted passages.
- Persistent storage in browser localStorage.
- My Notes page with search, chapter filter, color/type filter, edit, delete, and jump-to-source.
- Export/import annotation backups as JSON.
- Create personal flashcards from selected theory or existing annotations.
- Flashcards page now mixes course glossary cards with personal cards.
- Chapter Learn sidebar shows saved annotations and lets you jump to them.
- TextQuote-style anchoring uses selected text + nearby prefix/suffix so annotations are more resilient to future theory updates.
